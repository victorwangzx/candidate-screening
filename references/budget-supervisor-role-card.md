# Budget supervisor role card

Configurable starting point. The user's JD controls salary, location, reporting, experience, team, tools and business coverage.

| Dimension | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Annual budget and monthly breakdown | Ownership, assumptions, models, coordination, approvals and seasonality |
| Business understanding/modeling | Drivers, scenarios, profitability and resource assumptions |
| Variance analysis/rolling forecasts | Root causes, timing versus structural effects, assumptions and horizon |
| Cost and returns | Baseline, alternatives, realized impact and business-quality tradeoffs |
| Corrections/collaboration | Owners, actions, implementation and results |
| Data/tools | Excel, reconciliation, definitions, systems and quality |
| Cash-flow forecasts when in scope | Collections/payments timing, cash versus profit and responsibility |
| Stability/cycles | Continued ownership, complete annual cycle and next-cycle improvement |

Read [stability and cycles](stability-and-cycles.md). Distinguish system deployment from ongoing operating ownership. Large budget size does not prove individual leadership. Verify formula, period, scope and contribution behind accuracy or saving metrics.

Interview examples: complete annual cycle and next-year revision; challenging a business assumption; a wrong forecast and correction; configuration versus operating judgment; inconsistent data before a meeting; a personally built and verified model.

Salary and location can block progress separately from capability. AI, SQL and BI are preferences unless required by the JD.
