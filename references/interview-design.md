# Interview design

Use the JD, interview stage, available time and candidate uncertainties. Follow a supplied style without copying unrelated or sensitive personal questions. Build a selectable bank, mark priorities and suggest realistic timing.

Include relevant question types:

- Role understanding and motivation.
- Actual capability: personally owned project through goals, evidence, decisions, actions, outcomes and retrospective.
- Work habits: competing tasks, dependencies, communication and quality.
- Urgent problems: incomplete data, deadlines, conflicting requests or incidents.
- Hypothetical decisions: clearly fictional assumptions; observe requests for missing information, tradeoffs and authority.
- Collaboration: actual disagreements, resolution and escalation.
- Learning/new tools: what was learned, used and verified, including AI or automation when relevant. Lack of AI use is not automatic failure.
- Reflection: mistakes, failed initiatives and subsequent improvement.

Attach concise follow-ups and observation points. Do not show observation points before the independent response. Multiple sound approaches may exist; avoid one rigid “correct” answer.

Ask for personal scope, data source, period/denominator, options, decisions, implementation and observable results. Hypothetical answers prove reasoning only; seek actual analogues or authorized work samples using fictional data.

Record strong/partial/unknown evidence separately from fluency. Do not request proprietary employer files. Macro opinions or family/health information do not establish job capability or justify salary pressure.
